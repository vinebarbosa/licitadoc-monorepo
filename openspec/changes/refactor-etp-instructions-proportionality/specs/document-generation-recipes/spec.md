## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: ETP recipe instructions MUST enforce proportional and concrete analysis
The repository-managed ETP instruction asset MUST guide generated ETPs to remain legally conservative while calibrating depth to the object's complexity, risk, value, criticality, and operational impact. The instructions MUST discourage prolixity, semantic repetition, inflated institutional language, and automatic approval of the proposed solution. The instructions MUST require concrete administrative analysis where compatible with the object, including logistics, receiving, storage, kits, conference, execution controls, practical alternatives, operational risks, and proportional benefits.

#### Scenario: Simple object receives concise proportional guidance
- **WHEN** the backend resolves the ETP recipe for prompt assembly
- **THEN** the ETP instructions include explicit guidance that simple purchases, common goods, low-risk objects, or well-delimited demands should produce concise sections without artificial expansion
- **AND** the instructions still require the canonical ETP structure to be followed

#### Scenario: Operational analysis replaces abstract institutional repetition
- **WHEN** the ETP instructions are reviewed
- **THEN** they discourage repeated use of abstract expressions such as public purpose, institutional action, community integration, preliminary viability, and similar formulaic language
- **AND** they direct the model toward concrete administrative analysis such as delivery, receiving, storage, kits, checking quantities, execution evidence, quality controls, and practical operational risks when compatible with the object

#### Scenario: Alternatives remain strategic without invented facts
- **WHEN** the ETP instructions guide the analysis of alternatives
- **THEN** they include compatible options such as direct execution, third-party contracting, SRP, ata adhesion, single lot, parceling, ready kits versus internal assembly, centralized supply, scope reduction, standardization, and logistical simplification
- **AND** they forbid presenting any option as a completed fact when it is not supported by context

#### Scenario: Existing legal and anti-hallucination safeguards remain
- **WHEN** the ETP instructions are reviewed after refactoring
- **THEN** they still prohibit invented data, invented legal citations, simulated market research, unsupported prices, unsupported budget data, unsupported supplier attributes, DFD/TR/minuta structures, and unsupported operational details
- **AND** they still treat missing, empty, zero, `0,00`, `0.00`, and `R$ 0,00` values as absence of estimate rather than valid prices
